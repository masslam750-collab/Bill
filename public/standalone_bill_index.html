How to find 14-Digit Reference Number?

1. Check the top-left or top-center box of your paper bill.

2. Look for the label "Reference No" (حوالہ نمبر).

3. It consists of 14 numerical digits (e.g. 06 15454 0709308 U).

4. Enter all 14 digits without spaces in the input box.
